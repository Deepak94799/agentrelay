# Incident runbook

Severity 1 incidents (outage or data loss) page the on-call engineer immediately. The incident commander opens a dedicated channel and posts a status update every 30 minutes.

Severity 2 incidents (degraded performance) are triaged within 2 hours during business hours. Escalate to severity 1 if customer impact grows or no mitigation exists after 4 hours.

After every severity 1 incident a blameless postmortem is published within 5 business days with timeline, root cause and action items.
